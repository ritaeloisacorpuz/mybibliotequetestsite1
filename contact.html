Advisory

The MOD website is a sharing economy platform that acts as an intermediary between professional cleaners seeking part-time employment and households seeking quality cleaning service. Exemplary Cleaning Services are the service providers that recruit, train and deploy the Cleaners. These service providers exercise due diligence by requiring the submission of proper identification before the Cleaner profiles are posted on the website and deployed to booking MOD clients. However, MOD clients are advised to exercise conscientious supervision of the Cleaners for security purposes and to avoid altercations and misunderstandings.

MOD is a brand developed by Hawthorne Consultancy for Exemplary. Hawthorne provides the marketing, branding and sales campaign support for its client, Exemplary, but it is the service providers who are responsible for the manpower recruitment, deployment and quality control of the Cleaners.
