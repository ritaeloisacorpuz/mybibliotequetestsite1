About Us

The Biblioteque Mission

myBiblioteque is an eLibrary, ePublisher and eZine in one. But more importantly, it's a sandbox.

As an eLibrary, we offer access to curated literary work inclusive of a learning guide to help your children develop the love for learning and personal development. We also have eVideos in our eZine section that tackle school life to guide our teens and young adults in finding themselves and their place under the sun. We also have a section for you, the mothers, and how we can help you access the tools you need to raise intelligent, successful kids that are life-ready for work or business. And lastly, as an ePublisher, we help aspiring writers polish their work — be it an eVideo, Netclip or an eBook — and publish it in our website that has a ready pool of readers and viewers.

myBiblioteque.com is a website where our teens and young adults can develop important lifeskills and explore which skill they can best use to earn a living in their future careers or businesses. This website is about helping our kids find out who they are, discover what they are good at and learn how to best use it to live the life they truly love in a way that is uniquely theirs.

In that way, myBiblioteque.com is more than just a website, it's a sandbox in creating their own unique playbook in adult life. Here, our children can explore where they might want to go — not in reference to what other people are telling them or what pop culture told them is the definition of success — but based on their own individual uniqueness and specific interests.

Join us and let's build children's future together.
